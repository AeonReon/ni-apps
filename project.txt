# NI Apps

## What it is
A single page you can hand to someone you've just met. It lists the free apps
built here for people in Northern Ireland, each with its icon, a headline, a
short description, a longer drop-down, and a spoken description they can play.
Underneath, plain instructions for turning any of them into a real app icon on
an iPhone, an Android phone, or in the Mac Dock.

## Design
- Palette + fonts are Days Out NI's: sky #5FB1DD -> #3F8FCB, sun #FFCE3D,
  deep #1F5E8A. Poppins for headings, Inter for body.
- The hero is the Days Out NI headline image (Northern Irish monuments), which
  doubles as the first featured app and sets the tone for the whole page.
- Gradient-outline treatment on every card and tile, per-card accent var.

## Featured apps (order matters — Days Out is the door-opener)
1. Days Out NI            https://daysoutni.com
2. FuelFinder NI          https://fuelfinderni.vercel.app
3. Happy Weather          https://happy-weather-aeonreon.vercel.app
4. Conscious Parenting NI https://new-beginnings-livid.vercel.app

To add an app: add an entry to the APPS array at the top of app.js, drop a
512px icon into images/icons/, add its script to scripts/build-audio.py and
re-run it. Nothing else changes.

## Audio
Seven clips, all Kokoro voice bf_emma (British, friendly), generated locally.
Rebuild:  python3 scripts/build-audio.py --force
Needs the local kokoro-tts service up (PM2 `kokoro-tts`, port 8765) and ffmpeg.
Decision 2026-08-30: Kokoro permanently — not the user's own voice.

## Deploy
Vercel (aeonreon team), static, no build command.
Domain: the user is buying a fresh one (niapps.net was free at build time;
niapps.co.uk / .com / .uk were taken). Until then it runs on the Vercel URL.

## Gotchas
- In-app browsers (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, TikTok) have no
  "Add to Home Screen". app.js detects them and shows a warning at the top —
  this is the single biggest reason install instructions "don't work".
- Never add a buildCommand. Static PWAs on Vercel get stranded at UNKNOWN.
